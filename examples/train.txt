The small robot reads a book. The book tells a story about a garden.
In the garden a child plants a seed. The seed needs water and light.
Every morning the robot brings water. Every evening the child reads a story.
A bird sits in the tree and sings. The robot listens to the bird.
The child asks the robot to count the seeds. There are one, two, three seeds.
The robot writes the answer in a notebook. The child checks the answer.
A small plant grows beside the tree. The child smiles and brings more water.
When it rains the robot stays inside. It reads a book about the sun.
The sun returns and the garden is bright. The child opens the little gate.
The robot and the child walk home. They tell a story about their garden.
小机器人每天读书，也每天给花园浇水。
孩子数了三颗种子，小机器人把结果写在本子上。
