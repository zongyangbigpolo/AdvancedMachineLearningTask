Raw and cleaned Simple English Wikipedia article export

Data providers: Simple English Wikipedia contributors.
Source: https://simple.wikipedia.org/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
Full license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

The raw XML is a Special:Export snapshot of 16 named articles, not the entire
Wikipedia database. Per-article titles and exact revision URLs are in
data/wiki-export/source.json and reports/data-card.json. Article revision
pages link to the contributor histories and preserve attribution.

Change notice for train.txt, val.txt and *.bin:
This project removed templates, references, HTML markup, link syntax and
some table/section markup; normalized whitespace; filtered short, malformed
and duplicate texts; split by article; and encoded text with a byte BPE
tokenizer trained on the training split only. These are modified data.

The original raw text and these modified text/token data remain under
CC BY-SA 4.0. No additional restrictions are imposed on the data.
SHA256.json lists every bundled file and its digest.

Small .pt files are this project's independently trained experimental model
checkpoints. They are not weights obtained from the reference project.
