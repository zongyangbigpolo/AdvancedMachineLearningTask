Stage 5 agent experiment artifacts

The environment and synthetic demonstrations were generated by this
repository's experiment.py. The task operates on an in-memory integer ledger.

The neural policy starts from this repository's stage-four SFT seed-42
checkpoint. trajectory-sft.pt contains the imitation-trained policy;
agent.pt adds environment-reward training.
The pretraining-data attribution remains documented in stage three.

demonstrations.json includes ordinary and review prompts.
reports/traces.json includes observations, proposed actions,
executed tools, feedback and external verification.
reports/demo.jsonl is a fresh execution after reloading saved agent.pt.
SHA256.json lists every bundled file and its byte-level digest.
