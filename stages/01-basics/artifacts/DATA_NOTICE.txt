Change notice for data/week1-raw/train.txt and val.txt:
These files are modified subsets of TinyStories, not the original full dataset.
The first 512 training and 128 validation stories were selected from the
dataset-server API. Internal whitespace was normalized; each story occupies
one line. Source URLs, download-page hashes, transformation details and the
resulting text hashes are in data/week1-raw/source.json.

Change notice for data/week1-tokens/*.bin:
These are the same modified texts encoded by this repository's byte BPE
tokenizer, with an EOS token appended to each story. They are uint32
little-endian token IDs, not independently collected text.

All included data and modified data remain under CDLA-Sharing-1.0.
This archive adds no restrictions on use or redistribution.
The included tokenizer, metadata and logs document this experiment.
SHA256.json contains byte-level hashes of every bundled file.
