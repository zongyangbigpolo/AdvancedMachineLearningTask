Stage 4 alignment experiment artifacts

Task data: all ordered pairs (a, b) with a and b in 0..9.
Prompts ask max(a,b); the verifier returns 1 only for the exact correct digit.
The split and examples were generated by this repository's experiment.py.

All nine models start from the stage-three 289,248-parameter checkpoint.
The upstream pretraining snapshot and attribution are documented in
stages/03-data-scaling/artifacts/DATA_NOTICE.txt.

The policy uses the same ten-digit candidate mask for the base and every
method. These checkpoints implement the constrained max(a,b) task.
SHA256.json lists every bundled artifact and its byte-level digest.
